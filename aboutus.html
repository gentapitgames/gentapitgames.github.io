We are an independent game development studio based in Jaipur, Rajasthan, India. We aim to create fun, approachable and engaging mobile games that players can enjoy in their free time.

Our current project, Water Puzzle Quest, is a colorful liquid-sorting puzzle game. We are developing and testing it with a focus on straightforward gameplay and a friendly user experience.
