Water Puzzle Quest – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 November 2026 · GenTap Games

This policy explains what information the mobile game Water Puzzle Quest (“the app”, “the game”) handles, why, and the choices you have. The app is made by GenTap Games (“we”, “us”).

In short: you play without an account. Your game progress is saved only on your device. We do not collect, receive, or sell your personal information. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some information to show and measure those ads, as described below.

1. Information stored on your device

To save your game, the app keeps the following on your device only. It is never sent to us; we have no server and cannot see it.

  • Your progress: levels reached and completed, stars, best number of moves.
  • Your points balance and how points were earned or spent in the game.
  • Daily puzzle results and your daily streak.
  • A game in progress, so you can continue where you left off.
  • Your settings, such as sound effects, vibration, animation speed, accessibility options, and the bottle and background you chose.

This information contains no name, email address, phone number, or other personal details. If you have your device's own backup turned on (for example Android backup to your Google account), your device may include the game's data in that backup. That backup is run by your device and its provider, not by us.

2. Information we collect

None. The app has no account or sign-in, and it does not send your game data or any other personal information to us. We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC (Google Ireland Limited for users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland):

  • small banner ads on some screens; and
  • optional “rewarded” ads: when you do not have enough points, you can choose to watch an ad to receive in-game points. These ads only play when you tap to watch one.

To show ads, limit how often you see them, measure their performance, and prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads software in the app may collect and process information such as:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

Your ad choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where required): the first time you open the game, Google's consent form asks whether ads may be personalised. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings › Privacy Choices. If you decline, you may still see ads, but they are not personalised.
  • Advertising ID (Android): you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your device's settings (usually Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads).
  • Google's ad settings: you can manage how Google personalises ads at My Ad Center.

4. Permissions the app uses

  • Internet and network state – to load ads and the consent form.
  • Advertising ID (Android) – used by Google's Mobile Ads software as described above.

The app does not ask for or use your location (GPS), camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or files.

5. Selling and sharing

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not receive personal information that we could sell or share. Information collected by Google for advertising is described in section 3.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

  • Game data on your device stays until you delete it. You can erase it at any time with Settings › Reset Progress in the game, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Information collected by Google for advertising is kept and deleted under Google's policies (linked above). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID at any time.

7. Children

The game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us using the details below and we will help.

8. Security

Your game data stays on your device and is protected by your device's own security. Ad information is sent by Google's software over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe and the UK, or US state privacy laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not collect your personal information, most requests are about information held by Google; you can exercise those through the links in section 3 and your device settings. You can always contact us with questions.

10. Changes to this policy

If the game changes how it handles information (for example by adding in-app purchases or new features), we will update this policy and its effective date on this page.
